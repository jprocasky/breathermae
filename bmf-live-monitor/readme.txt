Live session count, who is on the site, and a rolling 24/48-hour activity graph — as shortcodes and Elementor widgets.

== Description ==

BMF Live Monitor tracks who is currently active on your WordPress site and exposes that data as shortcodes. Lite is enough to put a live count or dial on a dashboard. Pro adds guest tracking, extra gauge shapes (including a thin footer bar), colors, a history graph, and optional session-detail columns.
